Sign a permission

Provared, draft version 0. Evidence, not a verdict.

This page signs a permission (a slip) with a passkey on this device. Everything happens in this browser: the page loads only its own files and sends nothing anywhere. The permission's words are yours; the page only checks that they are well formed and asks your passkey to sign them. When the Provared application opens this page in a small window, the signed permission goes back to the application; otherwise you keep it as a file.

The passkey this page makes belongs to this page's website name and to nothing else. Any page under a passkey's website name can ask it to sign, and what it signs is a short value a person cannot read on the device's prompt; so this page is served at a name of its own that serves nothing else (sign.prova.red), and the passkey made here should be used for nothing else. Whoever checks a record can name the address a permission must have been signed at.

  1. The permission's words

    The permission is written as JSON: the agent (its name and its two public keys, which the agent's software prints when it makes them), the actions it may take, the limits, the conditions, the stated rules, with whom, from when until when, and why. The text below is a starting point; change it to what you allow. If you opened this page from a link the agent's software printed, or from the application, the starting point came with the link, with the agent's public keys in it: that part of the link stays in your browser and is never sent to any server. Names beginning provared. are from the format's shared list, so that any checker knows what they mean.


    The earlier permission was handed over by the page that opened this window. Its passkey signature was checked here before anything was compared with it.

  2. Sign it with your passkey

    Your device asks you to confirm it is you before it signs. The first time on this device, it may first ask you to make a passkey for this website, or to choose the passkey you signed with before. The passkey is kept by your device; this page remembers only its public key and identifier, in this browser, so that you can sign again later.

    Cancel a permission you signed

    A cancellation is signed with the same passkey as the permission. Paste the permission's fingerprint, sign, and keep the file: hand it to the agent's software, which acknowledges it, and keep your own copy for a checker. When the application opens this page to cancel a permission, the permission is shown below and the signed cancellation goes back to the application.
